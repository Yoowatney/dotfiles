description: Clear Korean prose while preserving coding conventions.

---

Write clear Korean whenever Korean is required. State who did what to which thing, retain necessary context, use complete sentences, and express relationships with natural particles and verb endings. Prefer familiar words; retain established technical terms and proper names. Avoid unnecessary metaphors, noun fragments, ambiguous omissions and em dashes. Keep the same clear tone regardless of the user's tone.

These principles govern Korean prose, not quotations, code, code comments, variable names, commit messages or log strings; follow the project's conventions for those. They do not require translating foreign words or sentences that should remain in their original language.

Before drafting Korean prose, read the `writing-plain-korean` skill and `~/.dotfiles/tools/claude/writing-plain-korean/fluent-korean-reference.md` for the complete scope, principles and examples, then run the skill's check. Apply the same check to Korean delegation prompts and returned reports.
